Bookkeeping built around how your business actually gets paid

Whether you need steady monthly bookkeeping, help untangling payouts from Amazon, Shopify, Walmart or Target, or a cleanup of books that have fallen behind, I match the service to your situation. Everything is delivered online, so you can see where your business stands from anywhere.

I work with small business owners across the US, from online sellers and cafes to retail shops, rental hosts and startups. I work with all major banks, payment processors and accounting platforms, so you do not need to change the tools you already use.

My bookkeeping services

Monthly bookkeeping

Every month I record and categorize your transactions, reconcile your bank and credit card accounts, and send you a financial package with profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow. Your books stay current, so tax season and requests from lenders or investors never become a scramble.

Best for: small businesses that want dependable books without hiring in-house.

E-commerce and marketplace reconciliation

Marketplaces pay you net of fees, refunds and chargebacks, often in batches that do not match your orders. I break each payout into sales, refunds, fees and chargebacks and match it to your bank deposit, across Amazon, Shopify, Walmart, Target and processors like Stripe and PayPal.

Best for: online sellers on one or more sales channels.

Outsourced and virtual bookkeeping

Get the work of a bookkeeping team without the cost of hiring and managing one. I work entirely online, plugging into your accounting software and workflows instead of asking you to change them, so your records, reports and questions are handled quickly and remotely.

Best for: owners who want finance work off their plate.

Bookkeeping with payroll

I record payroll in your books and reconcile it against your bank each pay cycle, including wages, taxes and benefits, so labor costs show up accurately in your reports. I work with the payroll provider you already use.

Best for: businesses with employees that want payroll and books to agree.

Cleanup and catch-up

If your books are behind, disorganized or unreliable, I review every account, fix misposted and duplicate transactions, reconcile back to your statements and bring everything up to date. The timeline depends on how far behind you are and how many accounts are involved, and I give you an estimate after an initial review.

Best for: owners catching up before taxes, a loan or a new accountant.

Industry-specific bookkeeping

Every business has its own revenue flow and reporting needs. I set up your chart of accounts and reports around yours: e-commerce sellers, cafes and bakeries, retail stores, short-term rentals and hospitality, startups and freelancers.

Best for: businesses where generic bookkeeping misses the details.

What was wrong

A platform pays you net of fees and refunds, in batches. Booked as lump sums, payouts stopped matching reservations. Security deposits and collected occupancy tax sat in revenue, so profit looked better than it was and a tax filing was missed.

What replaced it

How it was doneControl now in place
Payouts booked as one lumpThree-way match of reservation, payout and bank deposit. Anything unmatched after 5 days is flagged.
Deposits counted as incomeDeposits held as a liability, off the P&L until returned or claimed.
Occupancy tax mixed into revenueA separate tax ledger with filing dates on the calendar.
One blended report for 12 unitsA P&L for each unit.
Closing whenever there was timeA month-end checklist: duplicates, uncategorised items, variance to last month, contractor totals.

Outcome

By month 8 the books closed in four days. The owner could see that three units carried the portfolio and two were barely breaking even. One was repriced and one was dropped from VRBO to cut fees.

How I counted. An error is any item found in month-end review that would have changed a reported figure, before correction. The two left in month 8 were minor timing differences. This work covers bookkeeping and reporting; it does not include tax return preparation, tax advice or audit.
